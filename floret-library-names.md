Raw enumeration list for seeds.hues.flowers (variety-first flow, step 1). Names and crop groupings only — no descriptions, growing text, or images were taken.

- **Fetched:** 2026-11-16 (America/Los_Angeles)
- **Total seed variety names:** 565
- **Distinct crops (Library groupings):** 92
- **Source collection:** "Seeds A–Z" — https://library.floretflowers.com/collections/seeds
  - Page 1: https://library.floretflowers.com/collections/seeds (250 names)
  - Page 2: https://library.floretflowers.com/collections/seeds?page=2 (250 names)
  - Page 3: https://library.floretflowers.com/collections/seeds?page=3 (65 names)
- **Cross-check:** the separate "Dahlia Seeds" collection (https://library.floretflowers.com/collections/dahlia-seeds) was also read; its 4 names (Dahlia Bee’s Choice, Dahlia Cancan Girls, Dahlia Petite Florets, Dahlia Shooting Stars) are all already in the Seeds A–Z list, so nothing was added.
- **robots.txt outcome:** Fetched first, before any other page. `User-agent: *` → `Allow: /` — public product, collection, and page HTML is crawlable. Disallowed paths are transactional/private only (/admin, /cart/, /checkout, /account, etc.) plus filter/sort crawl traps (/collections/*sort_by*, /collections/*+*, multi-filter URLs), none of which were used. No crawl-delay is specified. Requests were made one at a time with pauses between them; no 429/503 was received. Sitemap used for discovery: https://library.floretflowers.com/sitemap.xml
- **Scope:** SEED collection only. The Library's bulb/corm/tuber/plant collections (Dahlias A–Z, Ranunculus, Anemones, Tulips, Daffodils & Narcissus, Bearded Irises, Peonies, Specialty Bulbs, Roses, Chrysanthemums, Lilacs, etc.) were excluded by collection and not extracted.
- **Method:** each name is the Library's own product title as displayed on the collection page, spelling preserved (including the Library's "Strawflower Pomegranate"). The crop for each name is the crop the Library's title leads with, which matches its per-crop seed collections. Where a product title is just the crop name (e.g. "Bells of Ireland", "Euphorbia"), the name and crop are the same.
- **Machine-readable copy:** floret-library-names.json (same data: crop, name, source_url per entry).

## Notes and flags

- **The Seeds A–Z collection mixes cut-flower seed with herb/vegetable/filler seed**, as the Library organizes it. Herb/vegetable/filler entries included because they sit in the seed collection: Tomato (7), Pea — garden peas (9), Ornamental Squash (54), Basil (4), Dill (1), Oregano (1), Shiso (1), Quinoa (1), Orach (1). **Included per the owner's ruling of 2026-11-16: nothing is trimmed.** Cut flower farms sell filler material, and the Floret Library is curated for florists, floral designers and farmer-florists, so Floret's curation is the inclusion test. These entries stay marked as herb/vegetable/filler for information only — the marking is not a trim queue.
- **Image alt text disagreed with the product title in three places**; the product title (the Library's displayed product name) was recorded:
  - "Iceland Poppy Colibri Salmonato" — photo alt text reads "Iceland Poppy Giant Peach".
  - "Iceland Poppy Colibri Sherbet Mix" — photo alt text reads "Iceland Poppy Sherbet Mix".
  - "Tomato Red Currant" — photo alt text reads "Tomato Currant Red".
- **Accents:** two titles carry accents in the title but not in the photo alt text; the accented title was recorded: "Phlox Crème Brûlée", "Sweet Pea Enchanté". Also "Foxglove Café Crème".
- **Dahlias in this list are seed-grown** (strains/mixes sold as seed, confirmed by the Dahlia Seeds collection) — not tuber varieties.
- No login wall, paywall, or missing collection was encountered; every page of the seed collection loaded fully.
