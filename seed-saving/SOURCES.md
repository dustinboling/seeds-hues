# Sources — Seed Directory grower content (INTERNAL ONLY)

Compiled 2026-11-17 for `instructions.json` (seed saving), `growing.json`
(growing guides) and `classifications.json` (life cycle + hardiness for all
93 crops). **Internal record only.** Per the owner's standing ruling, the
public site shows NO source attributions — no source names appear in the
content text of any of the three JSON files.

Rule applied throughout: a field exists only where a real source states the
fact. Viability/storage figures, isolation distances, temperatures and day
counts below are all as stated by the cited sources; where sources
disagreed, the wording in the JSON uses the defensible middle (noted per
crop where it matters).

## Seed-saving sources per crop (instructions.json)

- **breadseed-poppy** — SSE flower profile (insect pollination, 300 ft
  isolation, ~2 yr viability); Baker Creek / rareseeds.com (capsules brown
  and fully dry; seed pours from pores under the crown).
- **nigella** — SSE profile (insects, ¼ mile, viability figures conflict:
  SSE 5 yrs vs two home sources 2–3 yrs → written as "roughly 2 to 5
  years"); wintercyclingcongress2016.org, seedstation.com.au,
  limitlessgrowth.co (pod colour change, rattle, crush/shake method,
  bee crossing of colours).
- **pincushion-flower** — threshseed.com Imperial Mix page (harvest as
  seed begins to shatter, crush/rub, up to 3 yrs); rareseeds.com Ritz Blue
  and Fama White pages (squeeze/rub, winnow, insect crossing);
  growinginthegarden.com (hybrids don't come true).
- **echinacea** — UF/IFAS and Rutgers via completegardening.com (gloved-
  thumb test, harvest before shattering); seed-bank.ca (finish-dry 2–3
  weeks, species cross, 2–3 yrs); stonepostgardens.com (shake heads in a
  lidded can); Illinois Extension / USDA via completegardening.com.
- **sweet-pea** — empressofdirt.net (snip pods with 3–4 in stem, hang/
  spread, shell, 3–5 yrs, crosses possible); plantcarehowto.casa
  (viability); threshseed.com pea note + UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions
  (self-pollinating, isolation generally unnecessary); WSU C166
  ("pod bursters" — collect before pods pop).
- **larkspur** — threshseed.com Carmine Pink Larkspur page (rattle stage,
  dry ~2 more weeks, self-pollination possible but insects cross, seed
  poisonous; viability conflict: 50% up to 3 yrs ideal vs growersblend.ca
  1 yr → written conservatively as "sow fresh, within a year or two");
  WSU C166 ("seed holder" — collect whole stalks partly dry);
  thehampshireseedco.com (insect-pollinated, crossing occurs).
- **strawflower** — SSE profile (self + insects, ½ mile, 2 yrs);
  farmhouseandblooms.com (fluffy dandelion-clock centre, air-dry heads
  7–10 days); veggiegardenseeds.com.au (OP comes true if no shared pollen).
- **zinnia** — SSE grow-zinnia guide (self-pollinating, bags/cages,
  "several hundred feet or one variety per season", heads fully dry,
  thresh, fan winnow, dry ≥3 weeks stirring 1–2×/day); SSE profile
  viability conflict: 10 yrs (profile) vs ~5 yrs (extension chart + Ask
  Extension) → written as "about 5 years".
- **sunflower** — SSE profile (insects, ½ mile, 3 yrs; paper bag over
  head); Penn State Extension seed-saving basics (bagging vs birds);
  schooloftraditionalskills.com (hang 1–2 weeks, rub out, fan winnow);
  general sources for 1–3 yr declining viability and F1 caution.
- **cosmos** — SSE profile (insects, ¼ mile, 5 yrs); biologyinsights.com
  (single-layer drying can take several weeks, flick test, discard
  soft/green seed); SSE/general for the C. bipinnatus × C. sulphureus
  non-crossing note.
- **marigold** — SSE profile (self + insects, ¼ mile, 5 yrs); Illinois
  Extension via homefortheharvest.com (heads brown/papery/shriveled,
  pull apart, dry days to 1–2 weeks, hybrids won't grow true).
- **calendula** — SSE profile (insects, 700 ft); viability conflict:
  SSE 9 yrs vs home sources best 1–2 yrs/up to ~3 (farmhouseandblooms.com)
  vs extension-compiled chart 6 yrs (gardenbetty.com) → written as
  "about 3 years, sometimes longer"; unrulygardening.com (single layer
  3–7 days, snap test); thehampshireseedco.com (mostly self-pollinating).
- **cornflower** — farmhouseandblooms.com (furry centre, seeds roll out
  when rubbed, cure 1–2 wks); limitlessgrowth.co (bees cross colours,
  F1 won't come true, viability a few years / 3–4 yrs from weaker sources
  → written as "a few years"); WSU C166 ("closed pods/heads" class).
- **celosia** — SSE profile (self + insects, ¼ mile, 4 yrs; harvest when
  seeds dried within heads and begin dropping; cut heads, hang, winnow).

## Growing sources per crop (growing.json)

Backbone: Johnny's Selected Seeds key-growing-information and product
pages (johnnyseeds.com) for germination temperatures/days, sowing method,
spacing, harvest stage, days to maturity, life cycle and hardiness lines.
Per-crop additions:

- **breadseed-poppy** — Baker Creek (Giant Rattle / Hungarian Blue:
  annual, frost-hardy, germ 14–21 days at 60–65°F, surface sow/light,
  80–90 days, spacing 8–10 in); University of Wisconsin Master Gardener
  program (hardy cool-season annual, light frosts, self-sows, do not
  cover); SemiSauvage (fragile taproot, transplanting very poor).
  harvest_for_cutting OMITTED: the only sear-the-stem guidance found is
  for corn poppy (P. rhoeas) — not transferred across species.
- **nigella** — Johnny's key info (germ 10–14 days at 60–65°F, spacing,
  harvest buds coloured / pods firm); Baker Creek Miss Jekyll (hardy
  annual, frost-hardy, 65–75 days, succession every 2–3 weeks, can start
  indoors 4–6 wks, taproot sensitive). Germination light/dark claims
  conflict across sources (darkness vs light) — omitted.
- **pincushion-flower** — Johnny's Easy Cut-Flower guide (annual,
  90–100 days, successive plantings, harvest almost fully open or green
  seed heads); Johnny's data via Petal Back Farm profile (germ 10–12
  days at 65–70°F, cut at ¾ open, vase life 7–10 days); SuperSeeds
  (surface sow/light, start indoors 4–6 wks, spacing 8–12 in); Hoss
  (90–100 days from sowing). NOTE: Johnny's S. caucasica page is a
  different (perennial) species and was NOT used.
- **echinacea** — NC State Extension via bloomingexpert.com (perennial,
  Zones 3–8, self-seeds, goldfinches); True Leaf Market (start indoors
  8–10 wks, spacing 12–18 in, "2nd Year Maturity"); stratification
  sources (fall sow or 8–12 weeks cold/moist). harvest_for_cutting
  OMITTED (no source). See flag below — no site crop page.
- **sweet-pea** — Johnny's Elegance series (75–85 days from seeding,
  germ 14–21 days at 55–65°F, presoak, darkness, transplant 4–5 wks,
  spacing 4–8 in, pinch at 6–8 in, trellis); Plant Answers (~50 cool
  days needed; transplant a month before last frost); Transition Farm
  (harvest coolest part of day, 2–3 flowers open, vase 4–7 days, heat
  ends flowering); NC State poison listing via plantanswers.com.
- **larkspur** — Johnny's key info (germ 14–21 days at 55°F, pre-chill
  7 days at 35°F, direct seed ¼ in darkness, poor germination above
  55°F soil, transplant 5–7 wks at 55°F, spacing 4–6 in, support, vase
  5–7 days, fresh/dried harvest stages, slightly alkaline soil);
  Johnny's QIS products (80–90 days); Petal Back Farm (poisonous).
- **strawflower** — Johnny's key info (germ 7–10 days at 70–75°F,
  transplant 4–6 wks, light required, pinch, support, spacing 10–12 in,
  vase 7–10 days, harvest at 2–3 petal layers before centre shows,
  bright mornings, overmature shatters when dried, 75–85 days).
- **zinnia** — Johnny's (germ 3–5 days at 80–85°F / 5–7 at 70–75°F,
  transplant 4 wks, root disturbance warning, spacing 9–12 in, vase
  7–10 days, 75–90 days, harvest once stems firm, air circulation vs
  mildew); Floret wiggle test via grayfloral / homegrown-garden.
- **sunflower** — Johnny's (germ 7–14 days at 70–75°F, direct seed ½ in
  after frost, transplant 2–3 wks, succession intervals, pinch rules,
  spacing by type, days by type: ProCut 50–65, Sunrich 60–70, branching
  60–75; harvest tight or almost open).
- **cosmos** — Johnny's key info (germ 7–10 days at 68–72°F, transplant
  5–7 wks, direct sow above 60°F soil, pinch, spacing 9–12 in, harvest
  as petals open, deadheading, support netting); Johnny's products
  (C. bipinnatus 90–110 days, C. sulphureus 65–75 days).
- **marigold** — Johnny's key info (germ 4–7 days at 75–80°F, transplant
  4–6 wks / 8 wks for flowering packs, spacing by type, harvest fully
  open with tight centres; days: French 50, signet 60, African 70–90).
- **calendula** — Johnny's key info (germ 7–14 days at 70°F, direct seed
  ¼ in, successions 2–3 wks apart, transplant 4–5 wks, spacing 6–12 in,
  50–55 days, harvest ~50% open, edible petals not calyx/centres,
  frost-tolerant).
- **cornflower** — Johnny's key info (germ 7–14 days at 60–65°F, direct
  sow ~6 seeds/ft ¼ in, thin to 6–9 in, transplant 3–4 wks with cool
  nights 50–55°F, harvest ¼–½ open, edible, beneficial insects,
  65–75 days); Baker Creek (annual, frost-hardy, fall sowing survives
  snow); UF/IFAS Extension (hardy cool-season annual, reseeds).
- **celosia** — Johnny's Sunday Mix + Reprise pages (annual, germ 8–14
  days at 70–80°F, transplant 6–8 wks, light required, grow on at
  63–68°F, premature flowering from cold/short days, spacing 6–12 in,
  vase 7–14 days, harvest fully developed with firm stem, dried
  handling, pinch rules by type; days: plume 85–100, cockscomb ranges
  55–75 to 90–120 by variety); jcgardenseed.com (self-seeds).

## Flags / identity notes (read before publishing)

1. **echinacea has NO crop page on the site** (it is not among the 93
   catalog crops). It is included in instructions.json and growing.json
   under the key `echinacea` as briefed, and deliberately NOT in
   classifications.json. If a crop page is added later, the content is
   ready.
2. **soapwort** — classified as the cut-flower soapwort, Saponaria
   vaccaria (annual; supported by cut-flower grower sources, e.g. zone-3
   annual, direct seed fall/early spring). If the site's crop is instead
   Saponaria officinalis, the correct entry is Perennial, Zones 3–9.
   Needs a one-line confirmation against the catalog's variety list.
3. **silver-lace** — classified as Tanacetum ptarmiciflorum 'Silver
   Lace' (perennial Zones 8–10, often grown as an annual). The name is
   also used for Senecio cineraria types (also tender perennials grown
   as annuals), so the life-cycle wording holds either way.
4. **bee-balm** — classified as the perennial Monarda didyma/fistulosa
   (Zones 4–9 / 3–9). Lemon bee balm (M. citriodora), the other common
   cut-flower "bee balm", is an annual or biennial — if the catalog's
   varieties are citriodora, flip this entry.
5. **ornamental-grass** — a mixed category on the site; classified from
   the standard cutting type (Frosted Explosion, Panicum elegans —
   Annual, per Johnny's). Perennial grasses exist in the broader
   category.
6. **argentine-forget-me-not** — a trade name identified as Omphalodes
   linifolia (Venus's navelwort), listed as an annual in seed trade
   sources. Worth a variety-list confirmation.
7. **chocolate-lace-flower** — classified Annual per Johnny's Dara
   product page (the cut-flower crop); the wild species Daucus carota
   is biennial.
8. **salvia vs clary-sage** — the site lists both. `salvia` is classified
   as the annual painted sage (Salvia viridis, Johnny's Annual);
   `clary-sage` as the biennial Salvia sclarea. Consistent with the
   variety lists as understood; flip if reversed.
