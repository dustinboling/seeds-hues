## Surprises & overlaps

- **Windfall Flowers is Canadian but sits in the page's US section** — and it's a pastel-zinnia breeder-farm (the Dawn Creek/Blomma profile) that ships to the US. Out of scope for launch only because it's BC-based; first name for a post-launch/international pass, or a policy question for Dustin since it ships US.
- **Three of the nine new candidates are zinnia breeders** (Blomma) or zinnia-heavy (Peace Seedlings) — plus Windfall internationally. Zinnia breeding is where the small-farm energy on this list concentrates, alongside sweet peas (Half Moon's, Millay & Meadowlark, Lunaseed, Songbird).
- **Sunflower Selections is the breeder behind ProCut and Starburst** — the series that dominate our sunflower catalog — selling direct at retail. Arguably the single highest-value matching target on the page.
- **Peace Seedlings has no shop to crawl** — mail-order by handwritten list + check. If approved, it's a manual-only seller under the crawl policy.
- **Blomma mirrors Dawn Creek's model exactly** (yearly fundraiser, limited quantities) — episodic handling already exists in our rules for it.
- **Select Seeds was already on our borderline list** before this page; Floret's endorsement doesn't change its status — it stays flagged for Dustin's ruling.
- **Johnny's appears twice** (US + Canada sections) and is Floret's most effusive endorsement on the page — still OUT by Dustin's standing rule.
- Floret's page does **not** mention three of our six exemplars (Hudson Valley, Wild West) or 13 of the 20 research candidates — this list complements the earlier sweep rather than duplicating it; only 9 of its 32 US entries overlap our existing lists at all (12 counting the borderline pair + Select Seeds).

## Matching plan (once Dustin approves)

1. **Dustin rules first:** approve/reject the 9 new candidates; rule on the 8 flagged (and the 2 carried-over borderline names, Snake River + Swallowtail, plus Truelove's network flag).
2. **Terms read per approved shop** (sets crawl policy: feed / html / manual_only; Peace Seedlings is manual-only by construction; Blomma gets episodic sale-window handling like Dawn Creek).
3. **Crawl in specialty order** — deepest catalog overlap first: Sunflower Selections (ProCut/Starburst vs our sunflower pages) → 3 Porch Farm ('Ms. Marilyn' + sunflowers) → sweet pea shops (Half Moon's, Millay & Meadowlark) → Summertime → Jig-Bee → Three Brothers Blooms (pansies) → Blomma (when its fundraiser window opens).
4. **Match against the 619** through the ingest API only: exact/synonym matches on approved sellers can auto-publish; everything else lands in the review queue with its evidence quote. New farm-bred varieties the crawl surfaces (e.g. 'Ms. Marilyn', Blomma's breeding lines) enter as new-variety proposals — never auto-created.
5. **Re-check cadence** per the spec's staleness table; sold-out and closed-window states recorded, never hidden.
